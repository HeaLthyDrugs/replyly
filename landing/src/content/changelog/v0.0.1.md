### New Capabilities
- **Multi-Model Intelligence**: Native support for **Google Gemini** (Gemini 2.5 Flash), **Groq** (Llama 3.3 70B & 8B), and **OpenRouter** (Claude 3.5 Sonnet, GPT-4o, DeepSeek).
- **Tone Presets Engine**: Instant switching between **Professional**, **Casual**, **Witty**, and **Custom** prompt instructions.
- **Deep Thread Context**: Auto-reads parent post author, text, thread depth, and intent directly from DOM on X (Twitter) and LinkedIn.
- **BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)**: Zero monthly subscriptions. Store your API keys in your browser's local sandbox (`chrome.storage.local`).
- **Zero Telemetry**: All network requests connect directly from your browser to AI provider endpoints. No proxy servers, no databases, no tracking.
